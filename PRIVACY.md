# Privacy Policy for HaniShot

**Last Updated:** November 2, 2026

HaniShot ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information and data security.

---

## 1. 100% Local Processing & Zero Data Collection

- **No Remote Tracking or Analytics:** HaniShot does not collect, transmit, track, or share any personal information, browsing history, keystrokes, or media files.
- **Local Media Storage:** All screenshots and video recordings generated by HaniShot are processed entirely inside your browser and stored locally on your device via browser IndexedDB.
- **No Cloud Uploads:** No images, audio streams, or videos are ever sent to external cloud servers, third-party analytics platforms, or developers.

---

## 2. Permissions & How They Are Used

HaniShot requests only the permissions strictly required to perform screen capture and recording functions:

- **`activeTab` & `scripting`:** Used solely to capture screenshots of the current web page (visible, selected crop, or scrolling full-page) when you explicitly trigger a capture.
- **`tabCapture` & `offscreen`:** Used to record video and audio streams of the selected tab or screen, and perform local media encoding via the standard MediaStream recording API.
- **`downloads`:** Used to save your exported video chunks and screenshot image files directly to your device's Downloads folder.
- **`storage` & `unlimitedStorage`:** Used to persist your local user preferences (such as auto-split length, resolution, and countdown timer) and save recorded media in your local browser IndexedDB without storage limitations.
- **`notifications`:** Used to alert you when a long recording split finishes or when an optional extension update is available.
- **`contextMenus`:** Adds convenient right-click context menu options to quickly capture screens.
- **`alarms`:** Used for background timers and periodic checks.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & Selling of Data

- **No Third Parties:** We do not partner with or use third-party tracking, advertising, or marketing tools.
- **No Data Selling:** We do NOT sell, lease, trade, or distribute user data under any circumstances.

---

## 4. User Controls & Data Deletion

You maintain complete control over all data stored by HaniShot:
- You can view and manage all stored recordings in the **HaniShot Gallery** (`gallery/gallery.html`).
- You can clear all cached recordings at any time from the **Options / Settings** page by clicking **"Clear Local Cache"**.
- Uninstalling the extension completely removes all extension data and local database records from your browser.
