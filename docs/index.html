Features

Everything You Need for Screen Recording

✂️

Smart Video Auto-Split

Long recording sessions are automatically chunked into ready-to-play numbered parts (e.g. every 10, 30, or 60 min). Never lose a recording due to browser memory exhaustion.

🎙️

Dual-Channel Audio Mixing

Record computer audio and microphone commentary simultaneously. Web Audio mixer synchronizes streams in real time with high-quality Opus encoding.

📸

3 Screenshot Modes

Capture your visible viewport, drag a transparent focus crop overlay, or perform automated scrolling full-page captures with pixel-perfect stitching.

🎨

Annotation & Image Editor

Crop, draw arrows, add rectangles, highlight, and write text over captured images immediately before saving to disk or copying to clipboard.

🔒

100% Private & Local

Zero data leaves your browser. All videos and screenshots are stored directly inside IndexedDB on your machine. No accounts, no servers, zero telemetry.

📁

Built-In Media Gallery

Browse, filter, preview, and download your past video segments and screenshots at any time from an on-device gallery interface.
