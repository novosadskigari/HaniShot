Privacy Policy for HaniShot

Last Updated: November 2, 2026

100% On-Device Guarantee: HaniShot does not collect, transmit, store on cloud servers, or sell any personal data or browsing activity. All media remains on your computer.

1. Local Processing & Zero Data Collection

HaniShot is engineered with a strict privacy-first architecture:

  • No Analytics or Telemetry: The extension contains zero tracking code, zero analytics SDKs (such as Google Analytics or Mixpanel), and zero marketing beacons.
  • Local IndexedDB Storage: All video files, screenshots, and custom preferences are stored entirely in your local browser sandbox through the HTML5 IndexedDB API.
  • No External Server Communication: HaniShot makes no background network requests to third-party endpoints or developer servers.

2. Browser Permissions & Purpose

HaniShot requests only the permissions strictly required to perform local screen recording and screenshot tasks:

  • activeTab & scripting: Used solely to capture screenshots of the current web page (visible, crop area, or scrolling full-page) when triggered by the user.
  • tabCapture & offscreen: Used to stream video and audio from selected browser tabs and record them using the MediaRecorder API.
  • downloads: Allows direct saving of exported video files and screenshots to your Downloads directory.
  • storage & unlimitedStorage: Stores your local configuration (auto-split duration, default countdown) and allows high-definition video chunks to be saved locally without hitting browser quota errors.
  • notifications: Displays notifications when an auto-split video chunk is completed or when an optional update is available.
  • contextMenus: Provides quick right-click capture shortcuts.
  • alarms: Powers internal recording duration timers.

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do NOT share, sell, rent, or trade your personal data or captured content with any third party under any circumstances.

4. User Control & Data Deletion

You have full autonomy over all captured media:

  • You can view and preview all recordings anytime in the HaniShot Gallery.
  • You can clear all local storage cache at any time from the Options / Settings page.
  • Uninstalling HaniShot immediately deletes all extension databases and stored items from your browser.

5. Open Source Transparency & Contact

HaniShot is free, open source software. You can inspect the entire codebase, report issues, or contribute at:
